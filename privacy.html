Privacy Statement

Effective date: August 9, 2026

CrossCents is an early-stage product build. This page is a realistic starting draft, not a lawyer-reviewed policy — replace it with counsel-approved terms before handling real user data or money.

What this covers

This Privacy Statement explains what information CrossCents collects when you use our marketplace and payments platform, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to freelancers, companies, and visitors to crosscents.example.

Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, phone number, and — for company admins — organization details, collected when you sign up or verify your identity.

Profile and listing information: work history, skills, job postings, and applications you choose to add to the platform.

Payment information: bank account and payout details you provide to send or receive payments. Card and bank details are handled by our payment infrastructure partners — CrossCents does not store full account numbers on its own servers.

Usage information: pages visited, searches run, and actions taken on the platform, collected automatically to keep the service working and to improve it.

How we use information

We use your information to operate the marketplace and payments features, verify your identity before sensitive actions like withdrawals, communicate with you about your account and transactions, and improve the reliability and security of the platform. We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share information with identity verification and payment infrastructure providers strictly to deliver the service, with companies and freelancers as needed to complete a contract you've both agreed to, and where required by law. We don't share your data with advertisers.

Data security

Sensitive actions — signing in, withdrawing funds, linking a payout account — require identity verification, not just a password. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, though no system can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You can review and update your profile information at any time while signed in, and you can request that we delete your account and associated data by contacting us. Some information may be retained where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Changes to this statement

We may update this statement as CrossCents evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

Contact us

Questions about this statement? Reach out via our Contact page.
