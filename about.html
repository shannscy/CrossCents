About CrossCents

A marketplace for one-off and short-term work, built so identity and payment move together instead of trusting a wire transfer to a stranger.

What CrossCents is

Companies post the jobs they need done — a one-off project or a short-term contract. Freelancers build a profile, browse and apply. Once terms are agreed and the contract is signed, payment runs through CrossCents itself — no separate invoice, no waiting on a transfer that takes days to land.

Why we built it

Cross-border hiring is still harder than it should be — for a company trying to verify who they're actually paying, and for a freelancer waiting on money that takes days to clear a border. CrossCents puts verified identity and payment in the same place, so the trust and the money move together.

Shannon Sim

Founder, CrossCents
Shannon Sim is a tech and SaaS professional who has seen firsthand how quickly technology — and now AI — is changing the way we work.

She believes the future of work will be more global, flexible, and less tied to a single company or country. CrossCents was born from that belief: a platform that helps professionals find opportunities across borders, connect with companies, and get paid seamlessly.

Because as work becomes borderless, access to opportunity and payment should too.
